## What I'm Building

### [Payce](https://www.joinpayce.com/)
A movement-powered rewards platform that turns everyday physical activity into value. Users track their steps, join walking challenges, compete with friends and clubs, earn points, and unlock cash prizes and brand rewards.

### [Encirco](https://encir.co/)
A business formation and compliance platform designed to help people start and manage businesses in Nigeria. I contribute to its frontend engineering and product development.

### [Rydvert](https://www.rydvert.com/)
A platform connecting businesses looking to advertise through vehicles with vehicle owners. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion.

### [Cravely](https://cravely-orders.vercel.app/)
A cross-platform food ordering application built with React Native and Expo. It uses Supabase for backend services and integrates Paystack for payments.

### Other Projects
I also explore e-commerce applications, AI-powered products, IoT systems and interactive web experiences. These projects help me experiment with different technologies and understand how software works beyond the interface.
